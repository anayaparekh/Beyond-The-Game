Support for Different Situations

If you feel unsafe or are thinking about suicide

You deserve help right now.

If you are thinking about suicide or are worried about your safety, please reach out. You do not have to handle this alone.

  • Call or text 988 in the United States.
  • Chat with 988 online
  • If there is immediate danger, call 911.

You can contact 988 even if you are unsure whether what you're experiencing is a crisis.

Text 988
☏

If you just need someone to talk to

You don't need the perfect words.

You can start with "I've had a hard time lately" or "I don't really know how to explain it, but I need someone to listen."

  • Talk to a parent or trusted adult.
  • Talk to a school counselor, teacher, or coach you trust.
  • Visit Teen Line for teen support.
  • Text HOME to 741741 for Crisis Text Line.
Visit Teen Line
✦

If sports are becoming overwhelming

Your identity is bigger than your sport.

Pressure, burnout, injuries, playing time, team dynamics, and fear of disappointing others can become overwhelming.

  • Talk with a school counselor.
  • Ask a trusted adult to help you find a mental health professional.
  • Talk with an athletic trainer about what has been difficult.

You deserve support before things feel unbearable.

☼

If you're worried about a teammate

You can listen without fixing everything.

Let them know you care. Listen without judgment and help them connect with a trusted adult.

  • "Thank you for telling me."
  • "You don't have to go through this alone."
  • "Would you like me to sit with you while we tell an adult?"

Never promise to keep safety concerns secret. Get an adult involved if someone may be unsafe.

Not sure where to start?

Pick the safest adult you can reach today and tell them one honest sentence about how you've been feeling. You don't have to explain everything at once.

Outside the United States? Find a Helpline can help you locate support in your country.
